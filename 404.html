02 Process

How an engagement works

Four steps, a fixed price, and a decision date you know before you commit.

  1. 01

    A 20-minute call

    You describe what is slow, manual, or falling through the cracks. We tell you plainly whether we can help. If we are not the right fit, we say so on that call and point you somewhere better.

  2. 02

    Discovery week

    We map your systems, agree on what we are measuring, and write down what "working" means in numbers. You see the plan and the scope before you commit to a build.

  3. 03

    A 30-day pilot, fixed price

    Real software on your real data, running alongside what you use today. Your team trains on it while it runs, so nothing depends on a launch day going perfectly.

  4. 04

    Go or no-go

    On day 30 you get the numbers and a straight recommendation. Pilot fees credit toward production. If the system did not earn its keep, you walk away with no monthly commitment.

03 How we work

How we work

  • One team, start to finish. We design it, ship it, watch it run, and fix what breaks. No handoff to a support queue the week after launch.
  • The system refuses rather than guesses. If a payment, a stock count, or a customer message cannot be verified, the software stops and asks a person. You will never find out later that it invented an answer.
  • We tell you what is not ready. Integrations we cannot connect yet stay visibly switched off instead of faked. You always know exactly what you are buying and what is still ahead.
  • Nothing we build is a one-off. Our retrieval and agent components are maintained infrastructure, not throwaway scripts. That is why the second project ships faster than the first.

Not a dealership? We take on custom operator software on the same terms — same pilot, same fixed price, same go/no-go at the end.
