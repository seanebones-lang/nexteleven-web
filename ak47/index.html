02How it works

One frozen process.
Visible from end to end.

The release is deliberately legible. It does not continually retune itself or move the rules after seeing results. The owner chooses whether to run it, whether to enable live mode, and what positive integer contract count to prepare.

  1. 01
    Observe

    Discover the supported 15-minute market and receive market plus Bitcoin reference data.

  2. 02
    Evaluate

    Apply the identified confirmation-v2 rule. If the market does not qualify, nothing is attempted.

  3. 03
    Guard

    Check STOP, live mode, executable price, visible depth, current position, run identity, and endpoint time.

  4. 04
    Act or record

    Dry run records without ordering. Live mode can submit one owner-sized eligible entry in the window.

  5. 05
    Reconcile

    Retain order intent, fill, settlement, and unresolved state for review; send operating alerts to the owner's phone.

07Direct answers

Read this before requesting the packet.

The right buyer understands exactly what is being licensed—and exactly what is not.

Am I buying signals?

No. Available pings in the public Telegram group remain free. You are requesting information about private, self-hosted automation, the documentation and operating materials, and a named personal-use license.

Does it guarantee profit or a particular win rate?

No. A high observed hit rate can still fail to produce net profit. Entry price, fees, fill behavior, losing-contract cost, skipped markets, unresolved state, market regime, and drawdown all matter. No historical or observed result is a forecast.

Is it fully automatic?

After deliberate installation, preparation, dry-run verification, live activation, removal of STOP, and service start, it can submit an eligible wager without a second manual confirmation. The owner remains responsible for monitoring, account review, service health, STOP, funding, and live use. It is not a managed or “set it and forget it” service.

Can I choose the contract count?

Yes. The named owner may select any positive integer contract count for a newly prepared run by following the supplied procedure. Increasing count increases potential loss, fees, capital use, and required order-book depth. NextEleven does not recommend a count or wallet amount.

Does it wager every 15-minute window?

No. The frozen rule is selective. A market may not qualify, depth may be insufficient, the executable price may exceed the cap, STOP or another guard may be active, or a dependency may be unavailable.

Do I need Claude or a website?

No. Claude Desktop is an optional installation aid and the package includes a manual path. A domain, hosted dashboard, and mobile app are not required or included.

Can I share, resell, or turn it into a service?

No. Release 1 contains readable source under a named personal-use license. Sharing, resale, source publication, redistribution, white-labeling, SaaS, managed trading, team, fund, client, and third-party use are prohibited. The release is not machine-bound or cryptographically time-limited.

Are updates included?

Only maintenance specifically listed in the signed order form is included. New strategies, major versions, integrations, migrations, customer modifications, and future features are not automatically included and may be separately versioned and priced.
