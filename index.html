05 Process

How an engagement works

Four steps, a fixed price, and a decision date you know before you commit.

  1. 01

    A 20-minute call

    You describe what is slow, manual, or falling through the cracks. We tell you plainly whether we can help. If we are not the right fit, we say so on that call.

  2. 02

    Discovery week

    We map your systems, agree on what we are measuring, and write down what "working" means in numbers before you commit to a build.

  3. 03

    A fixed-price pilot

    Real software on your real data, running alongside what you use today. Your team trains on it while it runs, so nothing depends on a launch day going perfectly.

  4. 04

    Go or no-go

    On the agreed date you get the numbers and a straight recommendation. Pilot fees credit toward production. If it did not earn its keep, you walk away with no monthly commitment.

06 How we work

Principles that don't change by project type

  • One team, start to finish. We design it, ship it, watch it run, and fix what breaks. No handoff to a support queue the week after launch.
  • The system refuses rather than guesses. If a payment, a trade, or a customer message cannot be verified, the software stops and asks a person. You will never find out later that it invented an answer.
  • We tell you what is not ready. Integrations we cannot connect yet stay visibly switched off instead of faked. You always know exactly what you are buying.
  • Nothing we build is a one-off. Our agent runtime and retrieval infrastructure are maintained platforms, not throwaway scripts — that's why the second project ships faster than the first, regardless of discipline.

SaaS, fintech, AI integration, mobile, or dealership operations — every engagement runs on the same terms: fixed price, a working pilot, and a go/no-go date you know up front.
