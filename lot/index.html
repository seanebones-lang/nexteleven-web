What this replaces

Most independent lots run on a spreadsheet, a phone full of photos, a folder of half-finished paperwork, and one person who remembers the rest. It works right up until it doesn't — and buyers can tell.

Scattered inventory

No single answer for what's on the lot

Photos sit on somebody's phone, costs live in a spreadsheet, and VINs get passed around in text messages. Nobody can say what a unit actually cost you without asking three people.

Fragile paperwork

Every deal gets rebuilt from scratch

Tax gets calculated by hand. A voided sale doesn't put the car back in inventory. The invoice looks different depending on who typed it.

Lost numbers

Book-out values disappear

Your manager books a unit out, writes the numbers somewhere, and by the time you're pricing it those figures are gone. The decision gets made on memory instead.
