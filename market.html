04 Research & backtesting

Market Spot BTC

Autonomous spot BTC trader with broker-adapter architecture targeting Robinhood Crypto. Strict paper-first validation, immutable accounting journal, deterministic cost-equivalent benchmarks, and gated production-readiness roadmap. All live execution paths remain hard-disabled.

2026-08-16

G2.7 cost-equivalent passive benchmarks + journal integration

Completed G2.7 gate: every nonempty backtest now emits three deterministic comparators (Cash, Matched-notional buy-and-hold, Periodic DCA) derived from the immutable accounting journal rather than ad-hoc simulation.

  • Matched notional rule: Uses peak gross inventory cost basis capped by starting cash adjusted for entry fees. Guarantees apples-to-apples capital deployment vs. strategy.
  • Adverse slippage & fee model: Benchmarks execute at synthetic bar-open for buys and last-close synthetic bid with slippage for sells, exactly matching strategy fill assumptions.
  • Risk-adjusted metric: net P&L / max drawdown (undefined on zero-drawdown runs) plus explicit zero-drawdown status flags. No fabricated Sharpe/Calmar labels.
  • Hand-verified three-bar proof: Manual recalculation of a minimal 3-bar run confirmed journal round-trips, fee application, liquidation-to-flat, and benchmark parity.
G2 gate series now covers data quality (G2.1–G2.3a), accounting immutability (G2.5–G2.6), and benchmark parity (G2.7). G2.8 remains open for full research statistics and golden tests.
2026-08-15

Immutable accounting journal + G2.5/G2.6 contract

Introduced a single source-of-truth append-only journal that records every order, execution, partial fill, closed trade, fee, cost basis, and net liquidation value transition.

  • Separates gross realized/unrealized P&L, fees, marked equity, and final liquidation value per run.
  • Enables exact reconstruction of any historical run state without re-simulation.
  • Flat-to-flat round trip identification and open inventory tracking now deterministic.
  • End-of-data liquidation is treated as a real, fully costed sell fill that forces the run flat.
2026-08-12

Broker adapter + Robinhood target architecture

Refactored execution path into a clean broker-agnostic core with pluggable adapters. Robinhood Crypto (BTC-USD) is the primary live target; all other brokers remain future adapters.

  • Strategy, risk, ledger, and operations layers never reference broker-specific types.
  • Hard transport lock + CLI refusal prevents any live order submission until explicit gates pass.
  • Paper mode and backtest engine use identical fill and accounting paths as the future live adapter.
2026-08-04

Production-readiness roadmap (G1–G3)

Published controlling gate plan that sequences data fidelity, backtest correctness, research statistics, risk rails, and only then live authorization. Current status: G2.7 locked, live money authorization = None.

  • G1: data gate (completed, versioned 5-year Coinbase artifact with declared gaps).
  • G2: backtest fidelity + accounting + benchmarks (in progress, G2.7 done).
  • G3: risk rails, observability, and operator dashboards before any capital exposure.

This page is updated as engineering work happens. Sealed client briefs with full architecture diagrams, failure mode analysis, and production metrics available on request.

2026-08-17

G3.2e preflight blocked + support escalation

Current status: G3.2e read-only collector implemented and locally verified, but live preflight fails closed on Robinhood best-price row reporting ask below bid (contrary to v2 field definitions). Authenticated support case 021436592 escalated to back-end team. No cost evidence written; G3.3 paused pending protocol 2.0 redesign.

  • Detectability frontier (closed-form): minimum detectable annualized Sharpe for 27-month stitched OOS is 1.66 (m=1) to 2.56 (m=36) at 80% power / 5% FWER. 5-year G1 dataset requires 1.11–1.71.
  • Connection-failure review: crossed rows correctly terminate collection immediately (ValidationError path); retrying them would truncate the signed-spread distribution and bias the estimand. Refactor to separate payload / observation / admissibility policy is future work but must be behavior-preserving.
  • Live trading remains hard-disabled; no build can submit orders until gates pass.
Full status in README.md and docs/reviews/2026-08-17-*.md. Robinhood case remains the active dependency.
