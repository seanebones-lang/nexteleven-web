The problem

Parts revenue leaks when answers live in five places: the counter DMS, the other rooftop’s phone, an unread inbox, a spreadsheet, and memory.

Counter lag

“Do you have…?” takes too long

Customers speak cars and symptoms. Staff still dig SKU screens and multi-store stock by hand.

Email chaos

Inbox is the real CRM

Quotes, orders, complaints, and payment questions pile up without consistent grading or drafts.

Automation risk

AI without control is liability

Dealers will not buy unsupervised send or invented stock. HIL and fail-closed integrations are table stakes.
