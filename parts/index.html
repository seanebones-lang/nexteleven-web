Where parts revenue leaks

The answer a customer is waiting on already exists somewhere — the counter screen, the other store's phone, an unread email, a spreadsheet, or one person's memory. The cost is the time it takes to find it.

Counter lag

“Do you have it?” takes too long

Customers describe a car and a symptom. Your counter person has to turn that into a part number, then call the other store to check stock. Every step is another minute the customer spends waiting or calling someone else.

Inbox overload

The mailbox is your real CRM

Quotes, orders, shipping questions, payment disputes, and complaints all land in one place with no order of priority. The one that actually matters is somewhere in the middle of it.

Control

AI you can't supervise is a liability

No dealer is going to let software email customers or commit stock unsupervised — and they shouldn't. If your people can't review and override every action, it has no business on your counter.
