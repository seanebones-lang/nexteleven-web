Free play and ads

All game modes and tools are playable without a purchase. Occasional interstitial ads may appear at completed-run transitions when consent and age settings permit. Advertising availability varies. The game remains playable offline; ad and store services require connectivity.

Remove ads

Remove ads is an optional one-time store purchase, not a subscription. The intended U.S. price is $2.99; the purchase screen displays the current localized store price and applicable taxes. Restore on another device using the same Apple or Google Play account. Purchases are separate across stores. Resetting game progress does not refund or remove the purchase. Apple and Google handle payment and refunds under their applicable terms.

Local progress

Progress is local and may be included in device backups. There is no game account or developer-hosted cloud sync. Device loss, deletion, storage damage or backup changes can affect progress. Keep device backups according to your preferences.
