The game

Circuit Breaker is a single-player fictional puzzle game from NextEleven LLC. It is entertainment, not an AI security service or a guarantee about the real DeadBolt execution gate.

Free play and ads

The original 60-level campaign, Daily Breach, Containment and their tools remain free. FAILSAFE is an optional 60-level expansion with a three-level free preview. Occasional interstitial ads may appear at completed-run transitions when purchase status and required privacy choices permit. Advertising availability varies. Bundled puzzles work offline; ad and store services require connectivity. Optional rewarded refills are limited to one per campaign attempt and require the provider’s completion event. If no ad is available or it is closed early, the ordinary Resume and Restart choices remain available. No paid coin balance is offered.

Remove ads

Remove Ads and FAILSAFE are optional one-time store purchases, not subscriptions. FAILSAFE includes ad-free access; a standalone Remove Ads purchase does not include the expansion. The purchase screen displays the current localized price and applicable taxes. Restore on another device using the same Apple or Google Play account. Purchases are separate across stores. Resetting game progress does not refund or remove a purchase. Apple and Google handle payment and refunds under their terms. Product availability depends on the store and app version. A privately provided complimentary code unlocks FAILSAFE and ad-free play only on that device; it is not a store purchase and does not create cross-store ownership.

Local progress

Progress is local and may be included in device backups. There is no game account or developer-hosted cloud sync. Device loss, deletion, storage damage or backup changes can affect progress. Keep device backups according to your preferences.
