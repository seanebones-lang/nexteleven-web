ANDROID PRIVACY POLICY

CurioRound

Last updated October 26, 2026. This policy covers the CurioRound Android app from NextEleven LLC.

Your questions and progress

Daily scores, selected age range, saved answers and pack progress are stored on your device. CurioRound has no separate NextEleven quiz account and NextEleven operates no server that receives your quiz answers or library. The reviewed question packs are included in the app. There is no AI service or automatic content-upload feature. We ask for an age range, not a birth date; this Android version is intended for adults 18 and over.

Advertising and privacy choices

The free version includes opportunities for full-screen ads at completed-round breaks. Google AdMob supplies ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform manages required privacy choices. The app requests non-personalized ads, disables Google's publisher first-party ID, and removes Android Advertising ID and Privacy Sandbox ad permissions. These settings do not make advertising anonymous or eliminate all SDK information processing.

Google documents that its ads SDK can collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate an approximate location), app/ad interactions, diagnostic information, app set ID and other applicable device/account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google describes its SDK network transport as encrypted. See Google's SDK data disclosure, Google's privacy policy and the partners disclosed in Google's consent form. The app requests no GPS permission.

Where required, use Ad privacy choices in Packs to review or change your choice. A consent failure or unavailable ad never blocks the quiz result. The optional Remove Ads purchase stops the app from requesting or showing automatic ads after access is verified; it does not erase information previously processed by Google. Buying a question pack is separate from removing ads.

Optional Google Play purchases

Google Play processes payment and keeps purchase/account records under its own policies. CurioRound reads product identifiers, purchase status, tokens and signed receipts to verify and restore permanent access, and acknowledges confirmed purchases. A sealed receipt copy is stored locally to support previously verified offline access. The app does not receive card details. NextEleven operates no purchase-validation server.

Play Billing 9.1 and its transport components can send technical billing-operation results, diagnostics, session/other identifiers, app and SDK versions, device/build/OS information, locale/country and network metadata to Google for billing, reliability, measurement and security. This is separate from your quiz answers and may occur when the store checks access even without a purchase. Google controls its own retention and deletion practices.

Google Play Games on supported Android versions

Google Play Games on supported Android versions can connect your Google gaming profile. The SDK can authenticate an existing profile automatically; creating a profile remains your choice. Google processes gaming identity, including your gamertag and avatar where applicable, and service analytics and diagnostics for SDK reliability and improvements. Google documents HTTPS transport. This integration does not upload your answers, moves or local saves and adds no cloud saves, achievements or leaderboards. NextEleven does not request a server authorization code or store a player ID or token. Playing locally does not sign your Google account out.

The Android edition is intended for adults 18 and older. Versions with Play Games support initialize it after the existing adult audience choice; no date of birth is sent. Advertising privacy choices are separate. Manage profile visibility, game access and game-data deletion through your Google Play Games profile or Google Account. See Google’s Play Games SDK data disclosure.

Device storage and deletion

Automatic Android app-data cloud backup is disabled. Device-to-device transfer behavior can vary by Android version and manufacturer; CurioRound provides no in-app cloud sync or guaranteed transfer of progress. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can remove scores, saved answers, age choice, progress and locally cached receipts. Genuine Google purchases can be checked again using Restore with the appropriate Google Play account; deleting the app does not cancel a permanent purchase or delete Google's account/payment records. There is no NextEleven online quiz account to delete.

Source links, this website and support

Opening an answer's source takes you to that publisher, whose own privacy practices apply. Visiting our help pages sends ordinary web-request metadata to our hosting provider. Emailing support sends us the information you choose to include; please start with a description and avoid sending private receipts or account details unless needed.

Contact and updates

For support or privacy questions, email nextelevenstudios@gmail.com. We update this policy when practices change and show its date above.
