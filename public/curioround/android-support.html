CurioRound is a daily trivia game with explanations and links to the sources behind each answer. The Android release is being prepared.

What is free?

The Daily Five rotates through the 24 questions in Beyond Earth. Explore that collection, read each explanation, follow its source and save answers for later. The daily mix comes from this included collection. The day changes at midnight UTC, and an open round keeps the questions it started with.

After Dark: Halloween

Try five free previews. The optional one-time purchase unlocks the full 100-question edition, including those five previews, across ten themes: traditions, pumpkins, bats, spiders and insects, the Moon, eclipses, owls, monster stories, candy and spooky science. Progress saves after each answer; the next round favors unexplored questions from this edition. A pack purchase does not remove ads.

Ads and Remove Ads

Where enabled, an available automatic ad may appear at a completed five-question round break. There are no automatic ads on launch, between a question and its explanation, or as you leave. The app limits actual impressions to at least 90 seconds apart; ads may be unavailable. Remove Ads is a separate permanent upgrade. There is no subscription. Where required, Packs → Ad privacy choices lets you review your choices.

Google Play purchases

The app shows Google's current localized price before purchase. Purchases on Apple and Android are separate. Use Packs → Restore purchases while online and signed into the Google account that made the purchase. A pending payment does not unlock content; let Google finish it rather than purchasing again. Previously verified access can remain available offline. CurioRound has no purchase server, so it cannot promise immediate offline refund or revocation updates. Google handles payment and refund requests.

Your saved answers

Saved answers, scores and edition progress stay on this device. There is no CurioRound account or cloud sync. Automatic Android app-data cloud backup is disabled. System transfer behavior can vary by device; do not rely on it to preserve progress. Clearing storage, uninstalling or changing phones can lose progress and saved answers. Genuine Google Play purchases can be restored using the purchasing account; progress cannot be recreated from a purchase receipt.

Audience and sources

This Android version is intended for adults 18 and older. The app asks only for an age range, stored locally. A mistaken selection can be corrected. Source links open the original publisher outside the app; those sites have their own content and privacy practices. The Halloween edition includes folklore and historical monster-fiction references, plus an educational question about a blood-detection test. It contains no gore images.

Get help

Email nextelevenstudios@gmail.com with the app version, phone model, Android version and what happened. Please do not include passwords, access codes, private receipts or payment details in the first message. Read the Android privacy policy.

Google Play Games support

Android versions that include Play Games offer Google gaming sign-in. Local play remains available when sign-in is canceled or unavailable. This integration does not add cloud saves, achievements or leaderboards. Using guest play does not sign your Google account out. Manage your gaming profile and its data in Google Play Games settings. See this app’s privacy policy for SDK information processing.
