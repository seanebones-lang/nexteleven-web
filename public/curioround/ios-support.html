IPHONE & IPAD · VERSION 2.0 HELP

Keep your curiosity going.

This guide covers the prepared CurioRound 2.0 update to Facts. Check the App Store listing for actual release availability.

Start with the Daily Five

Today offers five questions with explanations and source links. The free Beyond Earth library has 24 questions; the daily set rotates through that finite library. It is not a promise of newly published questions every day. Explore opens the starter questions, and Saved keeps answers you bookmark. Earlier Facts favorites appear in Saved when their local records are readable.

Try After Dark

Packs includes five free Halloween examples. Those five are part of the full 100-question After Dark edition, not an additional 100. Purchasing the full edition unlocks five-question rounds, saved progress, missed-question review and replay. Progress belongs to this edition and saves after each answer.

Know what each purchase includes

After Dark unlocks the Halloween questions. Remove Ads separately removes automatic ads after access is verified. Both are permanent purchases with no subscription. The price on Apple’s actual checkout sheet applies to your storefront. Free questions and saved answers remain available without either purchase.

Restore and offline access

Use Packs → Restore purchases with the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Apple may ask you to sign in; never share that password or a verification code with support. A completed download and verified purchase can then be used offline. A first purchase or Restore needs a connection. Earlier paid Facts customers should use Restore for ad-free recognition; contact us if it is not recognized. Refunds or revocations can change access without deleting local progress or saved answers.

Ads and privacy

Ads are offered at completed-round breaks, not between each answer. An ad that is unavailable does not hold up play. Where required, Packs → Ad privacy choices reopens Google’s form, including for customers who purchased Remove Ads. This release is intended for adults 18 and over. An incorrect age selection can be corrected on the age screen.

Home Screen widget

Touch and hold the Home Screen, choose Edit → Add Widget, search CurioRound, and add the widget. The widget uses bundled questions; its refresh timing is controlled by iOS. Tapping a question opens its detail in the app. The widget itself does not display advertisements.

Before reinstalling

Do not uninstall as a first troubleshooting step: local progress and saved answers may be removed. Reopen the app, check that you installed the latest available version, and use Restore for purchase problems. Send the app version, device/iOS version, what happened and steps to reproduce to nextelevenstudios@gmail.com. Screenshots can help, but omit passwords and private account details.
