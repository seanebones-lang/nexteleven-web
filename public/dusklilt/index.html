Dusklilt / preview

A quieter place to end the day.

Prepared version 2.0 of DreamWeave brings offline sound, simple mixes, original stories and a private evening journal together.

About version 2.0. These pages describe Dusklilt, the prepared update to DreamWeave. Check your installed version and the App Store listing for availability. Earlier version 1.0 has different features and data practices.

Make a little room for quiet

Choose from twelve bundled recordings. Combine up to three sound layers, set their levels, and choose a timer and fade. Read six original short stories, or use optional on-device read-aloud with an available English system voice.

Playback starts when you choose it. No NextEleven account, external AI service or API key is required.

A complete free evening

All included sounds, three-layer mixing, timers, stories, notes, favorites, listening records and backup tools are free. Save one personal routine for free. Optional Plus is being prepared as a one-time purchase for additional saved routines; the actual localized Apple price will be shown before purchase when available.

There are no ads. Listening records describe app playback, not measured sleep, and Dusklilt makes no medical or sleep-treatment claims.

Keep your own records

Your journal, routines and history are stored on the device. Full JSON backups are available without Plus. They contain private notes and are not password-protected by Dusklilt, so choose storage and recipients carefully.

The same Apple app, thoughtfully reworked

The prepared update retains DreamWeave’s existing Apple identity. Original preferences remain untouched during migration. This is a preview of the update, not an announcement that it is available.
