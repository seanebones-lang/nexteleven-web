Dusklilt / support

A little help for your evening.

Support for the prepared Dusklilt 2.0 update to DreamWeave, by NextEleven LLC.

About version 2.0. These pages describe Dusklilt, the prepared update to DreamWeave. Check your installed version and the App Store listing for availability. Earlier version 1.0 has different features and data practices.

Start listening

Choose an included sound or ready-made scene and press Play. Open Listening to combine up to three sound layers, adjust their individual levels, and choose a timer and fade. Start at a comfortable device volume. Pause or stop whenever you need to; the app is not a medical device or a sleep monitor.

If playback cannot start, follow the displayed error rather than assuming a silent session is playing. Check your device volume and selected speaker or headphones. Contact support if the problem repeats.

Stories and optional read-aloud

Six complete original stories can be read without payment. Read-aloud uses an available English system voice on your device; it does not send the story to an external AI provider. Voice availability is managed by iOS and may vary. Use the reader’s Start read-aloud, Pause, Resume and Stop controls. Choose a reading pace before starting. Read-aloud stops the sound mix; leaving the story stops read-aloud. The sound timer applies to sound mixes, not the story reader. If read-aloud is unavailable, the complete text remains readable. Any voice downloads managed through iOS are subject to Apple’s settings and services.

What stays free

All twelve included recordings, mixing, timers, stories, journal notes, favorites, listening records and backup tools are free. Your first saved personal routine is free. Optional Plus adds additional saved routines. A routine keeps your selected sounds, levels, timer and fade together.

Plus and Restore purchases

The prepared new Plus offering is a one-time unlock. When available, Apple shows the actual localized price and confirmation before purchase. If the App Store cannot load purchase options, the free tools and existing saved records remain available.

Use Restore purchases with the Apple Account that made the purchase. The app also recognizes a valid matching historical DreamWeave monthly subscription, if one exists. Buying the one-time unlock does not cancel an existing subscription; manage any renewal through Apple Settings. Saved routines remain readable and playable if a historical subscription expires.

Your journal and listening history

Journal notes are personal reflections, not health assessments. Recent listening records describe sound-mix playback time. They do not include story read-aloud, establish that you were asleep, or measure sleep quality. Older DreamWeave planned-session data is retained separately and is not converted into measured sleep or verified listening time.

Back up before troubleshooting

On Tonight, tap the settings sliders in the top corner to open Yours to keep, then choose Export full backup. The JSON file can contain journal notes, routines, favorites, listening history and preserved older records. Dusklilt does not password-protect this export. Save it somewhere private and avoid sending it to support unless necessary and reviewed by you.

Import and merge backup adds new records while retaining the existing local version of a conflicting record. It does not provide automatic cloud sync or restore purchase authority. If saved storage cannot be read, editing is protected from replacing it. Keep the original file and contact support; do not delete the app before backing up.

Older DreamWeave data

The update keeps the existing Apple app identity and leaves original DreamWeave preferences untouched. Earlier story favorites and supported session snapshots are retained in the backup where possible. Migration warnings identify limits or unreadable old data; a preserved snapshot does not mean every old feature remains available.

Contact NextEleven

Email hello@nexteleven.dev with your app version, device and iOS version, and the steps that led to the issue. Please do not send passwords, authentication codes, card details or an unreviewed private backup. NextEleven is an AI-led software company; we assess support reports using the evidence provided.
