Product and purchase information for Dusklilt 2.0 and the upcoming 2.1 update to DreamWeave, provided by NextEleven LLC.

Released 2.0 · upcoming 2.1. Dusklilt 2.0 is the released update to DreamWeave. Version 2.1 adds bundled story narration and is pending Apple approval; its new features are not yet generally available. View the current App Store listing for the available version in your region. Earlier version 1.0 has different features and data practices.

App licence and Apple terms

Versions 2.0 and 2.1 link to the Apple Standard EULA. App licensing and purchases are subject to the applicable Apple agreement and purchase terms. This page explains the product’s behavior; it does not replace those terms or your applicable statutory consumer rights.

Free tools and optional Plus

All included sound recordings, three-layer sound mixing, timers, six story texts and optional system read-aloud, journal notes, favorites, listening records, backups and one personal routine remain free. The six bundled AI-generated story narrations in upcoming 2.1 are also free. Dusklilt Plus continues to add only additional saved named routines through a one-time nonconsumable purchase; 2.1 introduces no new recurring subscription. The actual localized price and purchase confirmation are shown by Apple when available; no fixed price is promised on this page.

The app can recognize an existing valid matching historical DreamWeave monthly subscription, if any. It does not claim that such a purchase exists for you. A one-time purchase does not cancel a subscription. Manage any renewal and request any refund through Apple under its applicable policies. Existing saved routines remain readable and playable after a historical subscription expires.

Your data and backups

Keep backups of important notes and routines. Export and import provide manual transfer, not automatic sync. Backup files contain your records, can include private notes, and are not password-protected by the app. Imports retain existing local versions when records conflict. A backup does not grant Plus; purchase entitlement is verified through Apple.

Audio and stories

The app’s owner identifies the sound-library recording sources as Pixabay. The separate story recordings in upcoming 2.1 are AI-generated narration, labelled Cedar or Marin in each reader. These recordings are incorporated into the listening experience; they are not offered as standalone audio downloads or paid raw sound packs. No ownership of stock recordings or clearance beyond their applicable licence is claimed. Original story text and native artwork are provided by NextEleven. In-app access does not grant a separate right to redistribute app content.

Version 2.1 bundles story recordings for offline play, pause, seek and replay. Apple system-voice read-aloud remains a separate option and depends on available iOS voices. The complete story text remains available if narration cannot run.

Use with care

Dusklilt is an audio and personal-reflection tool. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent a medical condition, guarantee sleep, or measure sleep quality. Listening records describe app playback. Choose a comfortable volume and use audio only where it is safe to do so.
