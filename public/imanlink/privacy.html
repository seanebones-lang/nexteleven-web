Privacy policy

NextEleven LLC · Updated November 5, 2026

This policy explains how ImanLink handles information when you use its prayer, Quran study and daily worship tools. ImanLink is free, with no ads, purchases or subscriptions.

Who is responsible

ImanLink is provided by NextEleven LLC. Contact hello@nexteleven.dev for privacy questions or requests.

Your local information

ImanLink stores your selected city and settings, bookmarks and reading position on your device. These records can reveal religious practice. ImanLink does not automatically send these records to NextEleven, and there is no ImanLink account to sign in to.

Location, direction and reminders

You can select a city without granting location permission. If you choose to use your location, ImanLink uses it to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction on the device. Compass readings are used on the device. Notification permission is optional and is used for the prayer reminders you configure.

Support and policy changes

Emailing support sends us the message and any attachments you choose to include. Send only what is needed to explain the issue. We may update this policy when app behavior changes; the date above identifies this version.
