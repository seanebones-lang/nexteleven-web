ImanLink support

NextEleven LLC · Updated November 5, 2026

Help with prayer times, Quran reading and daily worship tools. Every feature is free, with no ads, purchases or subscriptions.

Contact NextEleven

Email hello@nexteleven.dev. Include your device model, iOS version, ImanLink version and the steps that caused the problem. Screenshots are optional; remove private information first. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or private worship records.

Prayer times, Qibla and reminders

Start with the selected city, time zone, calculation method and manual adjustments. Local authorities may use different methods. For Qibla, move away from metal or magnets and follow the on-screen calibration guidance. For missing reminders, check the notification permission in iOS Settings.

Reading and bookmarks

Bookmarks and offline reading live on your device. For a content issue, report the chapter and verse, the selected translation and the app version. A reference is enough; you do not need to copy religious text into your message.

The same tools on Android

ImanLink is the iPhone and iPad edition of our prayer and Qur'an tools. The Android edition is Mihrab.
