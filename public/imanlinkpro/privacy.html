Privacy policy

NextEleven LLC · Updated November 5, 2026

This policy explains how ImanLinkPRO handles information when you use its prayer times, Quran reading and personal worship tools.

Who is responsible

ImanLinkPRO is provided by NextEleven LLC. Contact hello@nexteleven.dev for privacy questions or requests.

Your local information

ImanLinkPRO stores your selected city and settings, bookmarks and tasbih progress on your device. These records can reveal religious practice. ImanLinkPRO does not automatically send these records to NextEleven, and there is no ImanLinkPRO account to sign in to.

Location and direction

You can select a city without granting location permission. If you choose to use your location, ImanLinkPRO uses it to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction on the device. Compass readings are used on the device.

Purchases

ImanLinkPRO is sold through the App Store. Apple processes your payment and keeps the payment record; NextEleven does not receive your payment-card details.

Support and policy changes

Emailing support sends us the message and any attachments you choose to include. Send only what is needed to explain the issue. We may update this policy when app behavior changes; the date above identifies this version.
