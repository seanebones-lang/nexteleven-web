Help with prayer times, Quran reading and personal worship tools.

Contact NextEleven

Email hello@nexteleven.dev. Include your device model, iOS version, ImanLinkPRO version and the steps that caused the problem. Screenshots are optional; remove private information first. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or private worship records.

Prayer times, Qibla and tools

Start with the selected city, time zone, calculation method and manual adjustments. Local authorities may use different methods. For Qibla, move away from metal or magnets and follow the on-screen calibration guidance.

Purchases

ImanLinkPRO is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, refunds and purchase records under its own terms; contact Apple for billing questions and NextEleven for help with the app itself.
