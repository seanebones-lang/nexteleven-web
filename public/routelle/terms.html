Choose the plan that fits.

Routelle 2.0 is being prepared for release. The intended U.S. Plus prices are $2.99 per month or $19.99 per year. The store purchase screen shows the actual local price and full billing period before you confirm. There is no advertised free trial.

Free and optional Plus

Everyday free tools include up to five active habits and one routine, reminders, minimum efforts, a journal, basic progress and full backups, with starter guidance. Plus expands capacity and unlocks the full original programs and reading library, personal experiments, advanced reviews and PDF/CSV exports. Paid access is verified by the relevant store. Existing notebook records remain accessible when Plus expires; new premium creation may be restricted.

Recurring billing

Monthly and annual plans are auto-renewing subscriptions. You pay the full displayed period’s price, not a monthly equivalent of an annual plan. Apple charges your Apple Account; Google charges your Google Play payment method. On Apple, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to prevent renewal. Use the store’s subscription screen to view renewal dates and cancel. Cancellation normally stops the next renewal while access continues through the paid period, subject to refunds, revocation and store rules.

Manage, restore and refunds

Manage or cancel an Apple subscription · Manage or cancel a Google Play subscription.

Use Restore purchases with the same store account if access needs refreshing. Purchases do not automatically transfer between Apple and Google. Uninstalling the app, deleting your notebook or exporting a backup does not cancel billing. Refund requests go through the store that charged you; approval is determined by its rules.

License and privacy

The iOS app uses Apple’s standard end-user license agreement where no other app-specific agreement is shown by the store. Google Play use and purchases are subject to Google Play’s terms. See our privacy policy for notebook and purchase data.

Practical limits

Routelle provides organization and reflection tools, not medical treatment or a promise of particular results. Device settings can affect reminders and speech. There is no automatic cloud sync. Manual backups are not password encrypted. Contact hello@nexteleven.dev for app help; do not send passwords or card details.
