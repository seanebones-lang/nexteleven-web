Steady support

NextEleven LLC · Updated October 13, 2026

A little space to breathe, begin, and move at your own pace. Steady is provided by NextEleven LLC.

Contact us

Email nextelevenstudios@gmail.com for app help, accessibility feedback, or privacy questions. Include your device model, operating system version, Steady version, and a brief description of what happened. You do not need to share your tasks or check-ins. This inbox is not an emergency service.

Save or move your data

Open Settings & support, then Export my data. Keep the backup in a private location. On your other device, open Restore a backup and paste the complete JSON backup text. Check the preview before confirming that it should replace the data on that device. Steady has no account or automatic cloud sync.

Delete your data

In Settings & support, choose Reset local data and confirm Delete all my data. This removes the entries and preferences in this installation. Delete any exported files or device backups separately. Steady has no user account to delete. For deletion of support emails, contact the address above.

Make the app gentler

Settings & support includes Reduce motion and A quieter view. Reduced motion also follows your device preference. A quieter view hides totals. Timers are optional, and pausing or setting a task aside does not erase your progress.

Support beyond the app

Steady is a general self-help tool, not medical care. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent a medical condition. A qualified mental health professional can help you find support that fits. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. In the U.S. and Canada, call or text 988. Elsewhere, visit findahelpline.com.
