From the haul to the sale

  • Inventory with storage locations, notes and selected photos.
  • Allocate a haul’s cost across its items while retaining every cent.
  • Record selling price, buyer shipping, fees, postage, supplies and other entered costs.
  • Keep refunds, returns to stock, resales and corrections in a clear history.
  • Check a possible purchase against your own selling-price and fee assumptions.
  • Export a full workspace backup with photos, or JSON for record interchange.
An offline redesign

Version 3 replaces older online identification, market-price feeds and social features with a manual inventory and profit workflow. No AI key or app account is needed. Older saved records are retained separately for review and export; they do not enter the new totals until you confirm an eligible import.

Your app purchase includes the workspace

ThriftFlip 3 adds no new in-app purchase or subscription. Existing app customers do not need to buy the redesigned tools again. The App Store shows your local download price and current version before purchase.
