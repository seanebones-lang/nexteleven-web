WordLedger 2.1 for iPhone and iPad · Updated October 17, 2026

Your vocabulary stays on your device

NextEleven LLC publishes WordLedger, the next version of LexiGlow. Your saved words, definitions, examples, bookmarks, study decks, personal deck notes, discovery history and review schedule are stored locally. The app and its widget share this information through an Apple App Group on your device. WordLedger does not send that vocabulary to a NextEleven server, an AI provider or an advertising service. It includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDK and requires no WordLedger account.

Pronunciation and reference links

Pronunciation uses Apple’s system text-to-speech and installed voices; voice availability depends on your device settings. WordLedger does not record your microphone. Core vocabulary and practice work offline. If you choose to open an external usage reference, website or support link, that destination handles the resulting connection under its own policies.

Apple purchase services

Where available, Apple processes optional Plus purchases. WordLedger uses Apple StoreKit to read and verify purchase entitlements and previous app ownership on your device. NextEleven does not operate a purchase-validation server for this app and does not receive your payment-card details. Apple processes transaction and account information under its privacy policy. Existing eligible LexiGlow ownership can provide Plus after verification. Purchase availability depends on the installed version; purchase records are separate from local vocabulary.

Widgets, sharing and backups

Widgets may show words on your Home or Lock Screen. Recall mode hides the meaning but still displays the word. Use the system’s widget and lock-screen controls to choose what is visible. When you export or share a JSON backup or PDF study kit, you choose the destination. A backup can contain personal entries, review data and deck notes; keep it private. Recipient services handle shared copies separately. Apple device backups may include app data according to your settings. WordLedger does not provide its own cross-device sync.

Retention and deletion

Local vocabulary remains until you change or remove it, erase the app’s data or uninstall the app. Keep a backup before moving devices or removing the app. Exported copies and device backups have separate deletion controls. NextEleven cannot retrieve or remotely erase content stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app does not erase Apple’s purchase records or cancel a subscription purchased in an earlier version.

Diagnostics and voluntary support

Apple may process system diagnostics according to your device’s analytics settings. The app has no separate NextEleven telemetry service. If you email support, your email provider and our email provider process the message. We use information you choose to send to answer your request. Please use fictional examples or redacted screenshots and do not send passwords, purchase tokens, private deck notes or complete backups.

Contact and scope

Privacy questions: nextelevenstudios@gmail.com. This policy describes the native WordLedger iOS 2.1 update. Earlier LexiGlow versions can have different implementations. No Android release is covered by this page.
