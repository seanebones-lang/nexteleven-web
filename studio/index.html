04Process

From ambiguity to an inspectable handoff

The schedule follows the verified scope, dependencies, and acceptance conditions.

  1. 01

    Establish fit

    Clarify the owner, present failure or requirement, constraints, available evidence, and whether Sean is the right person to help.

  2. 02

    Write the boundary

    Agree on deliverables, exclusions, verification, access, communication, cost, and the concrete condition that ends the engagement.

  3. 03

    Deliver narrow increments

    Show working evidence early, state blocked assumptions, and keep unrelated feature ideas out of the active scope.

  4. 04

    Verify and hand off

    Provide the agreed checks, source or artifacts, documentation, known limitations, and a clear record of what remains.

05Boundaries

What NextEleven does not pretend to be

  • Owner-operated.Sean McDonnell is the accountable owner and primary builder. Any outside contributor is disclosed before involvement.
  • No guaranteed outcomes.NextEleven does not guarantee investment returns, revenue, cost reduction, adoption, rankings, lead volume, uptime, or other business outcomes.
  • Implementation is not adoption.Completed implementation, demonstrations, and tests are reported separately from deployment, client acceptance, production use, and business results.
  • Written commercial terms.Scope, fees, schedule, assumptions, ownership, support, acceptance, and cancellation terms are recorded in the signed agreement.
  • No client identity used as borrowed credibility.Confidential systems remain anonymized unless a client explicitly authorizes disclosure.
